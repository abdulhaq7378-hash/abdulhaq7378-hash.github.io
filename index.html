03 / Project

In development · Sep 2026

Guardian
Band.

A wearable that calls for help when the wearer can't.

People living alone can lie undiscovered for hours after a fall or a faint. GuardianBand watches motion and posture, decides when an event looks like a genuine emergency, and texts a caregiver — while giving the wearer a few seconds to cancel a false alarm.

Doc Concept notes Scroll to explore →
Hardware

Six parts, one job.

  • Arduino UNOController — samples, decides, drives outputs
  • MPU-60506-axis IMU — accelerometer + gyroscope over I²C
  • SIM900A GSMSends the SMS alert to predefined contacts
  • BuzzerAudible warning before any alert is sent
  • Cancel buttonWearer dismisses false alarms in the warning window
  • Status LEDsArmed · warning · alert sent

Detection

Not one threshold — four conditions.

Normal life is full of things that look like falls: sitting down hard, jumping, dropping the band. So the logic only escalates when several signals agree.

01
Free-fall dip below 0.4 g, then an impact spike above 2.5 g
02
Significant change in body orientation from the gyroscope
03
Stillness — no recovery movement in the following seconds
04
Warning window passes with no cancel press
Pipeline

From sample to SMS.

  1. 01
    SampleRead acceleration and angular rate continuously from the IMU.
  2. 02
    ImpactFlag a free-fall dip followed by a sharp acceleration spike.
  3. 03
    OrientationConfirm a large change in body angle.
  4. 04
    InactivityWatch for stillness and absence of recovery movement.
  5. 05
    WarnSound the buzzer. The wearer can press cancel.
  6. 06
    AlertNo cancel — SMS goes out to predefined contacts.
