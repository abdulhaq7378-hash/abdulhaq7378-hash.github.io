About

I study Biomedical Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology. My interest is the instrument side of medicine — how a scanner turns physics into an image, and how a small sensor on the body can recognise that something has gone wrong.

Projects

In development Embedded · Healthcare

GuardianBand

A wearable fall-detection and emergency-alert system for elderly and at-risk patients.

People living alone can lie undiscovered for hours after a fall or a faint. GuardianBand is a wrist or waist-worn device that watches motion and posture, decides when an event looks like a genuine emergency, and texts a caregiver — while giving the wearer a few seconds to cancel a false alarm.

The concept and detection logic are documented; hardware build begins this semester under faculty guidance. Figure 01 above is an illustrative simulation of the logic, not recorded data.
