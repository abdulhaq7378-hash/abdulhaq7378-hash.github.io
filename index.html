Why it matters

Help that arrives late turns a fall into an emergency.

People living alone may not get help after a fall, a faint or a medical emergency. GuardianBand is meant for the moments when nobody is watching.

  • Elderly people living alone
  • Balance disorders and frequent falls
  • Stroke-risk patients
  • Home healthcare monitoring
Keep scrollingSwipe for more →
Movement

It has to know a stumble from a fall.

Normal · ignore

  • Walking
  • Sitting and standing
  • Lying down on purpose
  • Hand and arm movement
  • Jumping, or dropping the band

Abnormal · act

  • Sudden hard falls
  • Slipping
  • Fainting
  • Slow collapse
  • Long stillness after a posture change

Detection

Not one threshold. Four conditions.

01
Free-fall dip below 0.4 g, then an impact spike above 2.5 g
02
Significant change in body orientation from the gyroscope
03
Stillness, with no recovery movement afterwards
04
The warning passes without a cancel press

Fig. 03 simulates the logic. It is not recorded data.

How it decides

Four states, one decision.

  1. State 1Armed

    Quietly sampling motion from the IMU. Green light.

  2. State 2Checking

    Something looked like a fall. It watches for recovery movement before doing anything.

  3. State 3Warning

    The buzzer sounds. The wearer has a few seconds to press cancel.

  4. State 4Alert sent

    No cancel. The GSM module texts the predefined contacts.

Status Concept and detection logic are documented. The hardware build starts this semester under faculty guidance.
